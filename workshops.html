Workshops

Hands-on learning in editing, cinematography, and storytelling.

Indie Film Collective is here to help you start your journey as a creative! We host Workshops on a regular basis, including:

  • So You Want To Shoot Your First Film - An Introductory class for those new to filmmaking
  • Cinematography & Lighting Masterclasses - A deep dive on how to set up the perfect shot
  • Post-Production: What Not to Miss - A class designed for newbies and seasoned creators, going over the important aspects of post-production.
  • How to Introduce Your Film - A discussion on the pros and cons of different methods for distributing your film.
  • And more!
